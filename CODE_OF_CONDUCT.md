# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making this project welcoming, collaborative, and respectful for everyone, regardless of background or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions
- Focus on constructive feedback and technical discussion
- Assume good intent, but communicate clearly when issues arise
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Keep discussions relevant to the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks of any kind
- Attacks based on identity, including nationality, ethnicity, race, religion, sex, or similar attributes
- Trolling, insults, or derogatory comments
- Dismissive or inflammatory language toward individuals or groups
- Sharing private or sensitive information without consent
- Disruptive behavior that interferes with collaboration

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, discussions, and pull requests
- Chat channels and community forums
- Public events or discussions representing the project

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:

- Warning
- Temporary or permanent restriction from participation
- Removal of contributions where appropriate

Enforcement decisions are at the discretion of project maintainers and will aim to be fair and proportionate.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to:

conduct@protorikis.com

All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
